## Apache License 2.0

The Apache License 2.0 gives you the freedom to work with the code with minimal restrictions. When using Velora, make sure to __include the copyright notice and license__, and __state any significant changes__ made to modified files.

Its appeal lies in its clear, well-defined terms and the additional legal protections it provides over simpler permissive licenses, making it a go-to for projects of any scale.

### Advantages

The Apache License 2.0 is favored for its balance between freedom and protection, appealing to those who find GNU/GPL licenses too restrictive but want stronger legal safeguards than the MIT license provides. Its broad wording supports both open community development and the use of components in proprietary software.

Here's what this means for you:

- __Freedom from vendor lock-in:__ Thanks to Open Source principles, you can use Velora without any strings attached. Customize or integrate it into your projects without worrying about restrictions. Your projects belong to you, and you are free to change the software at any time.
- __Explicit patent grant:__ Every contributor automatically grants you a license to any of their patents that cover their contributions, protecting you from patent claims when using the software.
- __Clear contribution terms:__ Unless stated otherwise, any contribution you intentionally submit for inclusion in Velora is licensed under the same terms, keeping the project's licensing simple and predictable.
